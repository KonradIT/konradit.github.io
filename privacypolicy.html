Osmosis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version. Osmosis does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Everything the app does happens on your phone and directly between your phone and your camera.

Osmosis (“the app”, “we”) is an independent, third-party Android client for copying photos and videos off DJI Osmo cameras and compatible devices. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by SZ DJI Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device, why, and what happens to that information.

1. Data we collect

None. Osmosis has no backend service and no user accounts. We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

To do its job, the app reads and handles certain information locally on your device. This information is processed only on your device and, where noted, sent only to the camera you connect to. It is never sent to the developer or to any external server.

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it goes
Photos & videos on the camera To browse, preview, trim, and download your media, and to mark or delete media on the camera. Between your phone and your camera; downloads are saved to your phone’s gallery.
Camera status To show battery, shooting mode, and storage use. Read from the camera; shown on your phone only.
Device location (GPS) Used for two purposes only, described in section 3. Stays on your device, except during optional GPS Sync, when it is sent to your camera.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth & Wi-Fi) To discover, pair with, and connect to your camera. Used on your device to establish the local connection.

3. Location permission

Osmosis requests location permission for two specific reasons. It does not use your location for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and it never transmits your location to the developer or any server.

  • Bluetooth scanning. On some Android versions, the operating system requires location permission before an app is allowed to scan for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices. Osmosis needs to scan in order to find your camera.
  • GPS Sync (optional feature). If you turn on GPS Sync, a foreground service reads your phone’s current location and streams it to the connected camera, so your recordings carry a GPS track that you can use later for telemetry overlays. This runs only while you enable it, the location is sent only to your own camera over the local connection, and it is not stored or transmitted anywhere else. A persistent notification is shown while the service is running.

4. Network access

When connected, the app communicates only with your camera at its local address (192.168.2.1). The app ships with a network security configuration that restricts its cleartext traffic to that local camera address. Osmosis does not contact activation servers, telemetry backends, or any cloud service.

5. Storage

Media you download is written to your phone’s gallery / shared storage so you can view and manage it like any other photo or video. Those files remain on your device and under your control. The app does not upload them anywhere.

6. Sharing and third parties

Osmosis contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Because the app does not collect any data, there is nothing to sell or share, and no data is disclosed to third parties.

7. Data retention and deletion

The app does not retain personal data on our side because it never receives any. Any settings and the list of your saved cameras are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Media you have downloaded stays in your gallery until you delete it.

8. Children’s privacy

Osmosis is a utility for camera owners and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

Because no personal data leaves your device or your camera, there is no server-side data store to breach. Connections to the camera are made over its local network interface.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s functionality changes. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and post the new version at this URL. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
